How much does custom software cost in South Africa?

Small automation tools start at R15,000 – R35,000, an MVP web app is R40,000 – R85,000 and a full business system is R85,000 – R180,000. A R2,500 discovery session produces a fixed quote and is credited in full if you go ahead.

Do I own the source code?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, the code, data and system are yours.

How are payments structured?

Software projects are 40% deposit, 30% at the midpoint and 30% on go-live, so you pay as milestones are delivered.

Can the software connect to tools I already use?

Yes — common integrations include email, Google Workspace, payment gateways like PayFast and Yoco, accounting software and WhatsApp.

What happens after launch?

You get a handover and can choose a monthly care plan or prepaid support hours for updates, fixes and new features.
