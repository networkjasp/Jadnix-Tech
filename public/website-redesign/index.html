Questions,
answered.

Will I lose my Google rankings if I redesign my website?

Not if it's done properly. I map every existing page to its new address and set up 301 redirects, keep your important content, and re-submit the site to Google Search Console after launch.

Can you redesign my site on the platform I already use?

Yes. The Refresh package keeps your current platform and gives it a new look, mobile fixes and speed improvements.

What if I want to move away from my current platform?

The Redesign + New Platform package moves your site to a new CMS or hosting, migrates your content and includes a training session for your team.

How long does a redesign take?

A refresh takes 1 – 2 weeks and a full redesign 3 – 5 weeks, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content is approved.

Do I own the redesigned website?

Yes. Your website, code and data belong to you once paid in full.
