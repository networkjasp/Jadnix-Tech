How much does a website cost in South Africa?

With Jadnix Tech a starter website of up to 5 pages is R4,500. A custom business website of up to 10 pages is R9,500 – R14,000, and an online store is R14,000 – R22,000. You get a fixed quote before any work starts.

How long does it take to build a website?

A starter site takes 1 – 2 weeks, a business website 3 – 4 weeks and an e-commerce store 4 – 6 weeks. The timeline starts once the deposit is paid and your content is supplied.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. The Business package and above include an easy way to edit your own pages, and a short handover so you know how.

Which payment gateways can my online store use?

PayFast, Yoco and Paystack are all supported, so your customers can pay by card, instant EFT and other local options.

How do payments for the project work?

Websites are 50% deposit to start and 50% before launch. Two design revision rounds are included.
