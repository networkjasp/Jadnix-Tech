Questions,
answered.

Should I use Zoho, HubSpot or a custom CRM?

If a standard sales process fits your business, a properly configured Zoho CRM or HubSpot is the quickest and most affordable option. A custom CRM makes sense when your process is unusual, you have many users and want to avoid per-user licence fees, or you want to own the system outright.

How much does a CRM setup cost?

Zoho or HubSpot setup is R6,500 – R15,000, with licence fees paid directly to the vendor. A custom CRM starts at R45,000 – R80,000.

Can you import my existing customer data?

Yes. Spreadsheets and contacts from other systems are imported and cleaned up as part of every setup.

Can the CRM connect to my website and WhatsApp?

Website forms and email are connected in every package. The Advanced custom CRM adds integrations such as accounting software and WhatsApp.

Is my customer data handled in line with POPIA?

The CRM is set up with user roles and access controls, and you remain the owner of your data. I'll walk you through the settings that matter for POPIA during handover.
